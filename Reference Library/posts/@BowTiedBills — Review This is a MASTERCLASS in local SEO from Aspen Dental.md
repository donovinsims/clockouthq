## Post

This is a MASTERCLASS in local SEO from Aspen Dental.

They opened an office in Merced, California a few weeks ago, and the page was built to rank from day one.

(Aspen had 5.2 million patient visits last year across nearly 1,100 offices.)

6 things this page does so well:

1) The page title is "Merced, CA Dentist Near Me | Aspen Dental".

Google reads that line first, so the service and the city go there, with "near me" on the end for the people typing exactly that.

2) Merced is in the H1 and 6 of the headings below it.

Google never has to uess which town this page is for.

3) It pulls its Google reviews onto the page.

There are 7 so far at 5 stars, and every one shown is dated October.

Patients mention the staff by first name, so a stranger reading them knows who'll greet them at the desk.

4) The FAQ answers what people ask right before they book.

"Does Aspen Dental accept walk-in patients, same-day or emergency appointments in Merced, CA?"

That's the exact question someone with a broken tooth types into Google, and the answer sits on the page with the city in it.

5) It handles the money question before the call.

You get a written cost estimate before any work starts, and they say 99% of in-office financing applications get approved.

6) The booking buttons are problems.

You tap "Broken Tooth/Tooth Pain" instead of digging through a services menu to work out what your problem is called.

Only thing I'd change is the hours.

Fridays close at 2pm and the weekend is shut.

Google shows the businesses that are open first, so a Saturday toothache goes to whoever's open.

A law firm or a med spa can copy all 6 of these this week.
