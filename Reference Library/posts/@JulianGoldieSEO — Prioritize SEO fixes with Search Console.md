## What the author recommends

The author sorts Search Console opportunities by query and page performance, including queries close to page one and pages with impressions but few clicks. The workflow picks an opportunity, makes a change and checks the results again.

## Codex assessment

The distinct contribution is separating query coverage from click-through problems so they can receive different fixes. The author reports ranking and traffic results and notes that the evidence cannot establish authorship or causation. The reported ranking timeline is specific to the example.

## Potential Clockout application

On an established site, inspect the page already receiving impressions and decide whether the query needs a better answer, a clearer title or a different page. Use business relevance and the search results to prioritize. Review changes before publishing.

## Possible test

Choose one query and its existing landing page. Log impressions, clicks, average position and the reason for the change, then compare equivalent periods after the change has had time to appear in search.
