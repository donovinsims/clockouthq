# My favorite SEO hack

## Post

My favorite SEO hack

- Go to google search console
- Performance > search results
- Add filter > query > custom (regex)
- Paste this: ^(?:\S+\s+){7,}\S+$
- It shows every search with 8+ words

These are the long, specific questions people google before they find you. it's the same kind of question they ask chatgpt

Pick the ones with high impressions and low clicks, and answer them at the top of the page that ranks
