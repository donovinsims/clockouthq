## Post

A useful service-area page needs more than a swapped city name.

Tristan adds five layers:

1. Branded proof that explains why this provider is different
2. Local context and nearby landmarks
3. Links to the relevant service cluster
4. Process details and FAQs
5. A Google Map embed

The page should help a local buyer understand the service and the place.

That is much stronger than cloning one template across every suburb.

## What the author recommends
A useful service-area page should include real differentiators, local context, links to relevant services, process/FAQ information, and an accurate map, rather than swapping the city name in a template.

## Codex assessment
Strong quality principle for future multi-page SEO work; the post itself is brief and provides no outcomes. A one-page offer may not need separate service-area pages.

## Potential Clockout application
Apply the anti-template principle to Clockout copy and only develop separate local pages when the business serves those areas and can provide distinct, useful information. This is a proposal, not an adopted business decision.

## Possible test
For any proposed service-area content, check whether each page contains distinct, accurate service and location details; merge or omit pages that cannot add useful local information.
