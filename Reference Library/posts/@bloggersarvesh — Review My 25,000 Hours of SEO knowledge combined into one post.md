## Post

My 25,000 Hours of SEO knowledge combined into one post:

(if you'd rather skip 14 years of trial and error and just rank - book a call at http://Alventramarketing.com)

1. GBP categories = ranking cheat code. Set the right primary category. Most people mess this up.

2. "My competitors rank high because they’re doing some shady stuff" - NOT TRUE.

3. Most local businesses don’t have a single page targeting their top 5 money keywords. Fix that.

4. You can’t rank in a city you don’t mention on your website. Sounds obvious. Almost nobody does it.

5. Your homepage should sell your main service first - not 20 things at once.

6. If your phone number isn’t clickable on mobile… fix that before anything else.

7. A 2-second longer load time can cost you 40% of your leads. Site speed matters.

8. You don’t need backlinks from Forbes. You need backlinks from your city.

9. Blog posts don’t convert. Service pages do. Prioritize accordingly.

10. Listings ≠ citations. Citations with correct NAP = gold.

11. Install call tracking software today. Thank me later.

12. Use real photos. Stock photos = Google and users hate you.

13. Every time you complete a job, ask for a review on the spot.

14. Make your GBP offer irresistible - discounts, free inspections, same-day service.

15. If your GBP service area is set to the whole country… you’re telling Google you serve nowhere.

16. CTA above the fold. Don’t make customers scroll to call you.

17. Add street names + neighborhoods you actually serve. Hyper-local relevance is what beats the big guys.

18. A GBP with recent reviews will outrank a GBP with more old reviews. Stale is dead.

19. Upload “before & after” photos weekly. Google loves fresh proof.

20. Internal linking = the cheapest power-up in SEO. Most sites barely do it.

21. Audit GBP every month: services, photos, offers, descriptions, posts.

22. GBP descriptions aren’t just fluff. Add keywords + benefits + offers.

23. Don’t let random people write dumb answers on your GBP. Own that space.

24. Add real pricing info. Transparency builds trust (and rankings).

25. If your phone number isn’t tappable on mobile, you’re losing calls daily.

That’s it!
