# Donovin lexicon and mechanics

Adopted from [Corey's attributed lexicon](../corey-ganim/lexicon.md). All frequencies and temporal observations there describe Corey. No measured Donovin lexicon exists yet.

Prefer concrete ordinary language: task, work, build, tool, input, output, check, follow-up, handoff, bottleneck, process, minutes, test, owner, workflow, assessment. Explain “agent” with the job it performs and its limits. Define an acronym before using it with beginners.

Retain contextual turns such as “the play,” “here's the entire model,” “full breakdown below,” “Huge,” and “extremely bullish” sparingly when the content earns them. These are optional reference expressions, not mandatory signatures. Never promise material “below” that does not exist.

Clockout's adopted method is Audit → Optimize → Automate. “AI Concierge” identifies a mentor offer / potential model in the business records; it is not a confirmed launched Donovin product. Do not use mentor brands as generic slang or Donovin CTAs.

Formatting: blank lines for thought changes, 1) or 1. for actual steps, dash bullets for components, concise labels ending in colons. Preserve contractions and varied sentence lengths. Short observations need no elaborate close; articles can sustain headings and detailed examples.

No blanket bans on optimize, framework, roadmap or leverage. Remove generic uses of “seamless solutions,” “revolutionize” or “unlock your potential” when they replace a concrete explanation. Avoid stuffing several reference catchphrases into a post. Casing and punctuation are editorial choices, not authenticity tests.

Numbers need a job: explain a process or transparent calculation. Label hypothetical inputs and estimates, and never turn a reference dollar amount or client count into Donovin evidence.
