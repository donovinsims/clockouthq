# Donovin positions

Explicitly adopted by the owner in the transformation brief. These govern substance over historical reference opinions.

1. **Sell outcomes, not AI.** Technology is a means; explain the problem solved.
2. **Diagnose before automating.** Audit → Optimize → Automate. Observe the process, simplify it, then choose the technology.
3. **Free diagnosis can lead to paid work.** Bounded Free Business Look → paid assessment when warranted → scoped implementation → recurring services when justified and reliably deliverable. Stages are optional; some problems go directly to implementation. No copied prices.
4. **Nontechnical people can learn to build useful things with AI.** Teach foundational skills for personal and professional use, with understandable examples and checks.
5. **Real relationships and practical demonstrations create opportunities.** Preserve founder-led local relationships without inventing conversations, meetings or conversion rates.
6. **Demonstrate and measure real value.** Distinguish actual measurements from estimates, hypotheses and demonstrations.
7. **AI agents should eliminate repetitive work.** Show a bounded useful job, review points, failure modes and exceptions.
8. **Practical AI education should be accessible.** Teach independence rather than dependence on a tool or personality.

## Explicit exclusions

No agency-startup education, coaching on selling AI consulting, agency client-acquisition lessons, consulting-income promises, in-person workshops as Donovin's offer, business coaching as the main product, or mentor financial results/products/memberships as Donovin's.

## Business versus personal identity

Donovin teaches practical AI, demonstrates useful technology and shares real learning. Clockout diagnoses problems, improves processes and implements appropriate technology. Initial local business geography does not restrict the personal brand.

A dated website-sales sprint does not make Clockout exclusively a website, chatbot, SEO or automation agency. Do not promote unverified service launches or use temporary proposed prices as permanent positioning.

## Argument structures worth retaining from Corey

Problem → specific move → mechanics → constraints → next action. Scene → details → observation, only with a genuine scene. Counterargument → evidence → practical alternative. Tool possibility → bounded application → testable value. Adopt construction, not Corey's claims or consulting-operator audience.
