There is a very simple AI service hiding inside every owner-operated business with an unanswered inbox.

You don't need a custom agent that can run the whole company.

You need one job:

Find a new inquiry. Figure out what the person wants. Draft the response. Make sure the owner sees it before the lead goes cold.

The implementation:

1. Collect the actual inquiries. Not made up examples. Pull the last month and group them by request type.

2. Identify which requests are safe to answer with a template and which need an owner.

3. Give the system access to approved business information. Services, service area, working hours, pricing rules and the correct booking link.

4. Have it draft responses for approval first. Don't give it permission to make promises, discounts or commitments it can't verify.

5. Log each inquiry, response, time to first reply and final outcome.

6. Review the exceptions every week. Add rules for the ones that repeat.

The first version isn't supposed to be impressive. It's supposed to be trustworthy.

Once it works, you can expand the job to follow-up and scheduling.

A business owner doesn't need to understand the underlying model to understand why fewer leads sitting unanswered is valuable.
