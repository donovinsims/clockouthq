## Exceptional outliers that must not become rules

- [2113243121358147638](https://x.com/coreyganim/status/2113243121358147638): exceptionally high views for a personal consumer AI-music opinion. Do not conclude that mentioning music is a durable performance strategy.
- [2078301190832664827](https://x.com/coreyganim/status/2078301190832664827): exceptionally high views for a reported billionaire conversation. Don't fabricate access to wealthy people or copy the anecdote.
- [2079692142646050995](https://x.com/coreyganim/status/2079692142646050995): blunt lowercase prospecting quote post reached far beyond typical. Its harshness works because it links to an actual reported story, not because caps and swearing create reach.

## What did not reliably work

- Weak posts still display the same rough vocabulary, punctuation and buyer focus. **Voice fidelity and engagement are separate problems.**
- Low views can reflect age, distribution and the context of the quoted source, especially for quote posts. Don't treat a failure to reach as a failure of writing quality.
- The 10 complete thread roots have fewer than 10 measurable view records, so thread-root ranking is not statistically supported; thread performance is tagged `unranked`.
- **11 posts with no views were explicitly tagged unranked**, not scored as failures. Replies are not supported by a clean, separate reply corpus, so no reply engagement claim can be made.

## Practical default

Favor a current, verified, useful business example that creates a tactical step list. Lead with the outcome, not a generic "AI is changing everything" assertion. Keep the writing grounded in creator-approved facts. Measure new output after publishing; none of this guarantees virality.
