## Recurring arguments

1. **Start with money and a pain point.** Which operational leak is costing time, losing leads or blocking revenue?
2. **Sell what the business actually buys.** Package diagnostics, a fix, coaching and reporting as distinct deliverables.
3. **Non-technical operators can sell and deliver useful AI workflows.** The skill is identifying the job, explaining value and maintaining quality.
4. **Show the exact model.** Pricing, step counts, software, outreach wording, frequency and how to measure.
5. **The next tool or distribution channel creates an opportunity only when connected to a real buyer and outcome.**
