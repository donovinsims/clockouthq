1.
AI Tools Assessment: $999
One 45-minute interview, recorded with Fathom. Claude analyzes the transcript in about 5 minutes. You deliver a templated report.
(you can get the template I use for free at
audittemplate.ai
)
Deliverable: 3 to 7 off-the-shelf tool recommendations scored on impact vs effort, plus a quick wins plan.
Guarantee: 5 hours back per week or 100% refund.
50 to 60% of these convert into implementation work. This is the front door. Everything below gets sold on the review call.

## Post 3 of 9

https://x.com/coreyganim/status/2102735820927193346

2.
Process Redesign: $3,500
There's actually no AI in this offer at all.
You map the current process, cut the redundant steps, and hand back three things: a current-state map, a future-state map, and a build blueprint.
Why it sells: automating a broken process just gives you faster chaos. Fix it first.
The blueprint is the shopping list for tiers 3, 4, and 5.

## Post 4 of 9

https://x.com/coreyganim/status/2102735833036173454

3.
Knowledge System: $3,000
One custom GPT (or Claude project) trained on the client's own materials.
A business brokerage client went from 500 buyer emails per listing to 10. Every email asked the same 4 or 5 questions, so the GPT trained on each listing's marketing package answers them.
One build. Runs on every future listing. That's why the price has nothing to do with the build time.
