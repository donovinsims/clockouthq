## Original text

I run 6 Grok Bots that fire 14 routines a week across 11 connected apps.

Here's the full breakdown of my top 10 use cases, including the schedule each one runs on.

Full video walks through every bot, skill, and connector inside my actual setup.

Run the business

1. Chief of Staff bot. The only bot I talk to. It delegates to the other 5 and catches any conflicts in my schedule.

2. Sunday week plan (skill) runs every Sunday at 8am. It plans my week in Todoist around my day themes (writing, video, sales, admin) using Google Calendar.

3. Second brain audit (skill) runs every Friday at 6:15pm. It compares the week's work across every bot against my second brain on GitHub and suggests edits so Claude and Codex stay in sync.

Grow revenue

4. Newsjack bot runs a morning scan of news stories and journalist requests. On day two it went through 243 media requests and had 2 pitch packs ready in my voice.

5. Connector bot runs every Friday at 8am.  It reads my Notion CRM, email, calendar, meeting notes, and DMs. Tells me who's due for a check-in, drafts it, and flags intros where both sides win.

6. Speed to Lead bot. Runs 3 different routines handling lead follow up, building call briefs, and nudging cold prospects when they go quiet for 2-3 days.

Make content

7. X Research bot runs every Monday and Thursday. It pulls trending posts in my niche plus my own outliers into a Notion database.

8. Analytics bot syncs my X, YouTube, LinkedIn, and Kit numbers into one Notion database.

9. My X article finder skill lets me give a bot a topic then it pulls 5 recent X Articles that performed well so I can study the titles and thumbnails.

Bonus

10. Facebook Marketplace bot answers buyers in Messenger, sets up the meetup or the sale, and preps shipping. It helps me turn garage clutter into cash.

Every use case is built from the same 4 blocks:

-a bot
-a routine (the schedule)
-a skill (the checklist)
-a connector (the apps)

The only bot that auto-sends anything is Facebook Marketplace Bot. It has a custom rule that allows it to override my "never send without my approval" setting so it can arrange sales without my involvement.
