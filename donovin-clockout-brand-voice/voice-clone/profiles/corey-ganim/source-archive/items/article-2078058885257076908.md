The Four Phases

Phase 1: The Discovery Call

This is a 45-minute Zoom call. But I'm not pitching solutions. I'm pulling problems.

The questions I ask:

Walk me through yesterday. Where did your time actually go?

What tasks do you dread but can't hand off?

Where does work pile up waiting on you?

What have you tried to automate but couldn't figure out?

The goal is to hear their pain in their words. Not what they think they should automate. What's actually eating their time.

I record everything with Fathom. The transcript is critical for what comes next.

Phase 2: The AI Analysis

After the call, I feed the full transcript into Claude with a specific skill we've built that does deep analysis. But you can get 80% of the results with a simple prompt like this one:

"Analyze this business owner interview. Identify 5-7 areas where existing AI tools could save significant time. For each opportunity, recommend specific tools and estimate weekly hours saved."

Claude catches patterns I miss. It connects dots across different parts of the conversation.

I review the output, add recommendations from my own toolkit, and cut anything that feels like a stretch.

Phase 3: The Report

I build the final deliverable in Claude Design. Clean. Professional. Looks like something a $10K consulting firm would produce.

The structure:

Executive summary with top 3 opportunities

Priority matrix (high impact + low effort = start here)

Tool recommendations with implementation details

4-day quick start plan

Financial impact breakdown

Next steps

Takes about 30 minutes to assemble from a template. Clients think it took days.

You can download my exact report template (for free) here.

Phase 4: The Review Call

This is where trust gets built and bigger deals happen.

I screen-share the report. Walk through each recommendation. Answer questions. Let them absorb it.

Then I ask:

Which of these feels most urgent to you?

Do you want to implement these yourself, or would you like help?

What's your realistic timeline?

About 60% want help with implementation. That's where the real revenue lives.
