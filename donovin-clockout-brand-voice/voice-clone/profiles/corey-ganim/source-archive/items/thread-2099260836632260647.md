## Post 2 of 11

https://x.com/coreyganim/status/2099260848636379258

1.
Partner with people who already sell an assessment
Ops consultants, CPAs, EOS implementers, recruiters.
They already run a diagnostic on every new client. Your AI assessment slots in as one more section of a deliverable they already sell.
The assessment is the easiest AI offer to white label.

2.
Build the co-branded one pager
Both logos on it. Their name on the intro. Your name on the fulfillment.
They send it to their client list. You do the work.
One page. It's the cheapest sales asset you'll ever make and it's the one partners actually forward.

## Post 4 of 11

https://x.com/coreyganim/status/2099260872363491547

3.
Skip the direct referral. Host the event.
Direct referrals are one client at a time, whenever the partner happens to remember you.
The higher leverage play: go to the CPA with a client list and say let's host a webinar or a barbecue for your clients. Split the bill 50/50. You talk for 15 minutes, I talk for 15 minutes on AI.
Almost no partner says no to a free event for their own clients.

https://x.com/coreyganim/status/2099260884409577580

4.
Get the intro as a group text
Don't wait for an email intro that never comes.
Ask the partner to put you both in a group text right there. One message from them: hey, this is Corey, I think he can help with X.
Hormozi's version is a selfie with the referrer sent to the prospect. Same idea. Make the intro happen while you're still in the room.

## Post 6 of 11

https://x.com/coreyganim/status/2099260896304562548

5.
Update the referrer every single step
I referred a lead to an AI consultant a few months ago. Never heard a word back. I have no idea if they even talked.
She'll never get another lead from me. Not because she did anything wrong. Because I can't tell if she did, and I look bad to the prospect either way.
Text the referrer: we talked, we're in discussions, we signed. It takes 10 seconds and almost nobody does it.

## Post 7 of 11

https://x.com/coreyganim/status/2099260908300341671

6.
Follow up more than feels comfortable
A friend mentioned an intro to me two months ago. I texted him about it roughly once a week. 7 or 8 follow ups.
After the fourth he said: dude, keep doing this, this is what it takes.
This week he made the intro.
Partners are busy. Your follow up is a feature, not an annoyance.

7.
Turn one venue into a recurring room
One of our community members asked his coworking space to host a free monthly AI lunch and learn. They said yes. Their sister location said yes.
He's booked through December. 4 sessions since July, 4 to 6 leads, one $2,500 paid workshop, another closing.
A venue is a referral partner with a room.

8.

My 2 key takeaways:
1.
Partners don't refer AI work because they don't know how to explain it. The one pager and the 15 minute event slot are you explaining it for them.
2.
The follow up is the product. Every partner is deciding, silently, whether you make them look good. Updates are how you win that decision.
