## Post 2 of 9

1.
company. md
What the company sells, who it serves, how it makes money, what it believes, what makes it different.
How to build it: paste your website, your last 3 proposals, and one sales call transcript into Claude. Ask it to draft company. md from those 5 sections.
10 minutes. It will be 80% right. The 20% you fix is the part that makes every future output sound like you.

## Post 3 of 9

https://x.com/coreyganim/status/2098536839338754480

2.
customer. md
ICP, pains, objections, buying triggers, questions they ask, the exact language they use, fears, decision criteria.
How to build it: this one comes from call transcripts, not from your head. Feed Claude 5 to 10 sales or discovery calls and tell it to pull the customer's words, not yours.
If your customer. md has no direct quotes in it, it's fiction.

## Post 4 of 9

https://x.com/coreyganim/status/2098536851107934226

3.
offer. md
Packages, deliverables, pricing logic, proof, promises, claims to avoid, what counts as a good fit customer.
How to build it: write it yourself first. This is the one file where your judgment beats the transcript. Then have Claude interrogate it: what's ambiguous, what's unpriced, what would a skeptical buyer ask.
Claims to avoid is the section everyone skips. It's the one that keeps your agents from overpromising on your behalf.

## Post 5 of 9

https://x.com/coreyganim/status/2098536862906507775

4.
voice. md
How the business talks, how it should never sound, phrases it uses, phrases it avoids, examples of good writing.
How to build it: hand Claude 10 pieces of writing you're proud of and 3 you hate. Ask for the rules that separate them.
The mistake: describing your voice with adjectives. Confident, friendly, direct. Useless. Voice. md needs examples and banned words.

6.
Keep them alive
Stale context is worse than no context, because the agent trusts it.
Set a weekly routine: read this week's transcripts and drafts, suggest edits to the 4 files, wait for approval.
The files should change a little every week. If they haven't changed in a month, your business has and they haven't.
