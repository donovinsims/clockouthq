The Grok Bot setter we run on our free community (works for any business with inbound leads):

The trigger:

-Someone new comes in (free community, email list, etc)
-Bot sends them a DM written in our actual typing style, imperfections included
-One message, one free offer

The offer:

-Something personalized they get by filling out a short form
-Ours is a custom scaling plan. Yours could be an audit, a quote, a teardown, etc.
-We add 2 specific questions to the form: "want it by text?" and "interested in working with us?"

The reply loop:

-The bot checks for replies every hour
-It categorizes each reply and drafts a response into a Notion queue
-Every draft gets a status: Pending, Approved, or Revised
-Simple replies ("thanks", "send it over") get auto-approved
-Anything marked Revised, the bot studies what we changed and updates its own rules

The follow-up:

-No reply gets a follow up at 3 days, 7 days, and 1 month
-They reply: they exit follow-up and go back into the reply loop

The weekly dashboard:

-DMs sent
-Replies
-Response rate
-Free offers requested
-Free offers delivered
-Conversion to paid after DM
-Conversion to paid after free offer

The hourly reply checker burns through Grok But usage quickly so we're having our Dot do this part of the process since it's usage isn't currently charged.
