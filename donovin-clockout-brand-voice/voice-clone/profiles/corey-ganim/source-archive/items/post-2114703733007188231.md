I built a Grok Bot that acts as en elite fitness coach.

It includes:

-My full athlete profile
-A complete exercise library
-In-depth training and nutrition playbooks based on @Mindpump's public methodology

(Mind Pump is my favorite fitness podcast, the hosts have 20+ years of training experience and they 100% know their stuff. This Bot is trained on all their publicly available knowledge).

I worked out the kinks then turned it into a free Grok Bot template called Fitness Coach.

Install it and it:
1. Interviews you, one question at a time
2. Builds your profile and a starting training block
3. Sets your calories, macros, and meal timing
4. Writes your first session
5. Coaches each workout and logs your top sets
