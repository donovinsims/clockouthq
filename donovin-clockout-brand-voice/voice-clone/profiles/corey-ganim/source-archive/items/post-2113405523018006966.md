## Original text

As a founder it’s easy to get tunnel vision on the wrong things.

So I like to run the prompt below once a month or so to get an outsider’s view (the outsider being Claude) on what I should be focusing on.

The prompt:

Analyze my last 60 days of emails, call transcripts, todoist tasks, and any other necessary connector to tell me exactly what I should:

-keep doing/double down on (what’s working)
-stop doing entirely
-any other interesting insights you think I should be aware of that I may be oblivious to because I’m so in the weeds of my business on a daily basis
