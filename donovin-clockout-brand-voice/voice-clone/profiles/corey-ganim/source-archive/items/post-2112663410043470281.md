## Original text

My top 10 Grok Bot use cases so far:

1. Chief of Staff bot. I delegate to it, it hands work to my other bots, and it catches conflicts like a call overlapping my flight or a podcast invite with no recording link.

2. Sunday week plan. Every Sunday at 8am it plans my week in Todoist around my day themes (writing day, video day, admin day) and reschedules tasks to fit.

3. Nightly second brain audit. Every weekday at 6:15pm it checks the day's work against my two second brains on GitHub and suggests edits. Nothing changes without my approval.

4. Newsjack Bot. Every morning it scans the news for stories I can comment on, drafts pitches to reporters, and sweeps media request platforms. On day two it had 2 pitch packs ready and had triaged 243 media requests.

5. Connector Bot. It reads my Notion CRM, email, calendar, meeting notes and DMs. Every Monday it gives me the 5 people due for a check in with a draft for each, plus 1 or 2 intros where both sides win.

6. Speed to Lead bot. It sweeps my inbox for money threads like warm leads and live deals, then drafts the follow up so nothing goes cold.

7. X Research bot. It finds what performed on X in my niche over the last 14 days and notes why each winner worked.

8. Analytics Bot. Every week it syncs the last 7 days of my posts and email campaigns into one Notion database with live metrics.

9. Facebook Marketplace bot. It sells the random things sitting in my garage on Facebook Marketplace and answers buyers for me.

10. Article finder skill. I give it a topic and it pulls X Articles that did 5x their author's median bookmarks, with titles and thumbnails for inspiration.

Anything that talks to clients or important contacts is a draft. I still hit send.
