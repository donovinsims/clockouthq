## Original text

I know data brokering is a hot topic on X right now and a lot of people think it's a bubble that has a short runway but hear me out:

Right now the frontier labs are the primary buyers of this data, but the buyer pool is about to get a lot bigger QUICKLY.

Think about all the Fortune 500 companies currently building/training their own proprietary models.

These are companies more than willing to spend millions on private data to save months/years in training time to get an edge over their competitor's models.

Right now the labs are buying VOLUME of data, soon the value is going to shift to QUALITY of data.

Niche data will be worth a fortune to the right enterprise training their own models. Stuff like:

-CAD files
-insurance claims and adjuster notes
-legal case files
-HVAC and plumbing service tickets
-manufacturing QC logs
-clinical trial records

This space is one I'll be paying attention to. Things are about to heat up.
