## Original text

9 free Grok Bot templates you can install as a service this week:

All 9 are in the xAI marketplace. Here's what each one does, with a link to the template.

Full video walks through how I'd set each one up for a client and find businesses that need it (using @treg_ai).

Pipeline bots

1. Outbound Prospecting: builds a list from your ideal customer profile, researches every name on the public web, and drafts the first touch for you to approve. Runs a weekday draft batch, a Friday recap, and a Monday list top-up.
https://t.co/TlW27AnqGR

2. GTM Prospecting: enriches the list, skips anyone already contacted, and learns your voice from emails you've already sent. Has a humanizer skill so drafts don't read like AI.
https://t.co/PtM2q3RVkW

3. Lead Pipeline Desk: every morning it scores new inbound leads, merges duplicates, assigns an owner, and flags what's stuck. Sends a pipeline report every Monday.
https://t.co/1BebFPkbmL

4. Call Follow-Ups: checks the calendar every 30 minutes, turns each recorded call into a follow-up email in your voice, and updates the CRM. The promise tracker catches every "I'll get back to you on that."
https://t.co/YWqFCFGPxP

Insight bots

5. Paid Media Report Desk: turns Google Ads, Meta, and LinkedIn exports into one weekly report with commentary, and answers ad questions in Slack.
https://t.co/giOfOGskLJ

6. Market Researcher: watches competitor sites, ads, pricing, and positioning, then suggests how to counter them.
https://t.co/XchyRNHYcz

7. Talent Discovery: reads an open role, sources a daily batch of candidates who aren't already in your pipeline, and drafts the outreach.
https://t.co/drjuqzn4nO

Enablement bots

8. Sales Call Coach: scores every sales call and tells you what to fix before the next one. You can even have it drill you on objections by voice.
https://t.co/mlQ3RL6V9M

9. Customer Proof Desk: mines call notes and transcripts for case studies, testimonials, and proof points most businesses never use.
https://t.co/xpvwTB7hjG

And yes, the templates are free. Technically anyone can click "Import Bot."

But out of the box, none of the bots know the business's ICP, voice, or approval rules.

Business owners are willing to pay good money for that setup (and ongoing coaching/maintenance) as a service.
