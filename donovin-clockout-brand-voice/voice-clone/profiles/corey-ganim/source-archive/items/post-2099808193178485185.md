## Original text

The exact 2-sentence prompt I used to hire my Chief of Staff Grok Bot (bookmark this):

"You're my Chief of Staff bot. I want you to give me a list of all the things that a typical chief of staff would be responsible for, then suggest a system prompt that you can install for yourself."

Then add three lines of your own:

-who you are and what your business does
-what you want to accomplish inside Grok Bot
-"suggest the first bots you'd hire and what each one owns"

The bot writes its own system prompt. Then it hires and briefs the rest of the team. You never train the sub-bots yourself.

I don't guess where one bot's job ends and the next one begins. I let the agent tell me.

A friend went the other direction and had Claude Code build his Grok Bot team for him. 5 bots, system prompts stuffed with instructions, nothing ran. We deleted every one of them and started over with this prompt.

Sometimes you think you need 4 bots and 2 will do the job.
