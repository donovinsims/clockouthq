## Original text

If you want to get clients, make connections, build trust, etc...

You NEED to be hosting in person events.

This is my 4th month in a row hosting a local in-person AI For Business meetup in my city (Charlotte, NC).

I've made great friends, built valuable connections, and established myself as a local AI thought leader in 4 short months.

These are the exact steps I took to launch my meetup, and I highly encourage you to copy my process in your city/town:

1. Define your target audience. Mine is non-technical business owners. There are lots of meetups for devs, none for non-technical folks.

2. Pick a name for your meetup. I called mine AI For Business so people know exactly what they're getting by hearing the name. Don't overthink it.

3. Book a location to host the meetup. I rented a room at a local coworking space.

4. Create a registration page on Luma or Meetup. Make it clear what someone will take away from attending. Be specific.

5. Market your event in local business owner Facebook groups and by DMing people on LinkedIn/texting people direct invites.

6. Prepare a brief demo/presentation. At my first one I shared an Excalidraw diagram explaining what Skills are and how to build them. At this month's event we built a Brand Voice skill live on my computer. Again, don't overcomplicate this step.

7. Follow up with everyone who attended, ask what they liked/disliked then incorporate that feedback into the next one.

A few additional tips:

-Your first event may flop. It's ok, this is a long game. Keep doing them once a month and it'll grow.

-Find a well-connected person to co-host the event with you. The first one I ever did I co-hosted with a realtor. She invited all her friends/clients.

-Most people know nothing about AI. When you prepare your presentation, dumb it down then dumb it down again. Keep things SIMPLE.

Hopefully this helps you get your own local AI event/meetup off the ground. It has been a very fulfilling venture for me and my plan is to grow it exponentially and keep running it for years to come.
