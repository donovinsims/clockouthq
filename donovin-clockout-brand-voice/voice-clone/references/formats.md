# X Formats

These are defaults for when a profile is silent. The profile's own numbers always override them.

## Short post

One idea, one reason to stop scrolling. The hook is the post, so there's usually nothing after it to carry weak openers.

- Default length: 1 to 4 lines. Check the person's real range.
- Spend the effort on the first line. Most readers only get that far.
- End where the idea ends. Don't append a lesson unless the person does.
- Specific beats clever: a real number or scene outperforms a clean slogan.

## Thread

The first post is a standalone ad for the rest. If it only works as a setup, it won't spread.

- Each post carries one beat. If a post makes two points, split it.
- Open a loop in the first post and close it by the end, so there's a reason to keep reading.
- Vary post length. A thread of identical blocks reads as machine-made.
- The last post lands the payoff. It doesn't recap, and it only asks for follows or reposts if the person actually does.
- Typical range is 5 to 10 posts. Check the person's real range.

## Article

Longer, slower, and read by people who chose to open it, so the pace can breathe. But the first paragraph still has to earn the second.

- The title is the hook. Test it like a short post.
- The first paragraph states the stake or the story, not the topic.
- Every section should answer "why keep reading?" with a new turn, a number or a scene, not more of the same point.
- Use the person's real structure: do they use headers, bold, lists, images? Copy that.
- End the way they end. Many strong articles stop on the last real point.

## Long post

A single post past 280 characters. Treat it as a short post with room: the hook still does the work, and the extra length is for one idea developed, not several ideas stacked. Use the person's real line-break and paragraph habits.

## Quote post

The quote is the setup and the comment is the post. The comment has to add something the original didn't: a counterpoint, a sharper claim, a real example, a consequence. Restating the quoted post is the most common failure. Check how this creator handles it. Some agree, some argue, some joke.

## Reply

Shorter, more casual and more reactive than their main posts. Use their reply data (`replies.jsonl`) for this format, never their post data. A reply should sound like the person talking to someone, not broadcasting.
