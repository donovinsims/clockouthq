# AI Tells

Check every draft against this list before delivering. Each tell is here because readers have seen it hundreds of times and now read it as "bot."

Important: the profile wins. If the real person uses one of these, it's part of their voice. Keep it at their real frequency and no more.

This list is the universal starting point. Every rule should end up creator-specific: the "Never" list in each profile's `voice-dna.md` is built from the tells that appeared in the cold explore drafts and in later edits, and that list outranks this one.

## Structure

- **"It's not X, it's Y."** The contrast reveal. Reads as formula because it is one.
- **Tidy triads.** Three parallel items, every time. People's lists are uneven.
- **The neat closing line.** A moral, a callback, or a punchy summary. Most real posts just stop.
- **Rhetorical question, then the answer.** "Why does this matter? Because..."
- **Setup colon reveals.** "The result: ..."
- **Uniform rhythm.** Every sentence about the same length, every paragraph about the same size.
- **Restating the point at the end.** The post says it once, then says it again.
- **Perfectly symmetrical lists** with the same grammar in every item.
- **Em dash in every other sentence.** One is fine if the person uses it. A pattern of them is the tell.

## Phrases

- "Here's the thing" / "Here's the kicker" / "Let that sink in" / "Read that again"
- "In today's fast-paced world" / "at the end of the day" / "it's worth noting" / "let's dive in"
- "game-changer," "unlock," "leverage," "navigate," "landscape," "tapestry," "robust," "seamless," "elevate," "delve," "crucial"
- "Most people don't realize..." as an opener when nothing specific follows
- "Thoughts?" or "Agree?" as an automatic ending

## Emotion

- Manufactured vulnerability: a confession with no real stakes
- Fake humility: "I'm no expert, but..." before a confident claim
- Emotion stated instead of shown: "I was devastated" with no scene behind it

## Content

- Claims with no number, name or place
- Advice with no scene that proves it
- Generic examples ("a founder I know," "a client of mine")
- Lessons that could be pasted under any other post unchanged

## The pass

Read the draft once as a person who sees 200 AI posts a day. Mark anything that makes you think "bot." Rewrite those spots, then check that the fixes didn't introduce a different tell. Swapping one banned phrase for a near-synonym doesn't help. Change the structure.
