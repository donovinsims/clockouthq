# Proposed introductory website offer
As of 2026-10-31. Donovin changed the price. U02 directly supplies these proposed terms, refining the S01 recap and secondary offer. No customer contract, sale or payment is established.

**Test offer:** $500 one-time for one tightly scoped, mobile-friendly local-business page. Suggested terms: $250 upfront before substantive implementation; $250 after approval, before launch. No required retainer. The price is not permanent Clockout policy.

## Proposed included scope
- One responsive page with business-specific messaging and essential service information.
- A clear call to action and working contact links or an agreed configured contact method.
- Basic title/description metadata and readable structure; no ranking guarantee.
- Domain connection with client cooperation and authorized launch.
- One bounded revision round, defined in the written scope.
- Handoff clarifying code, domain, hosting and future responsibility.

## Exclusions unless separately scoped
E-commerce, custom booking, accounts, multiple pages, dashboards, complex integrations, extensive copywriting/content acquisition, new branding, paid stock assets, unlimited revisions and ongoing maintenance. Third-party domain/hosting/SaaS costs are outside the $500 fee and must be explicit.

## Ownership and commercial controls
The client should own the domain and hosting accounts directly; agree any exception explicitly. Record renewal cost/responsibility and access transfer. Do not make the client indefinitely dependent on Donovin's personal accounts.

Agree price, deposit/balance gate, acceptance criteria, revision boundary, delivery dependencies, cancellation/refund terms, rights, support and launch authority before payment. Do not automatically call a deposit nonrefundable. Delivery timing remains a per-engagement agreement: the secondary's 2–3 business-day aspiration is not an adopted guarantee.

## Sell before full build
Research and qualify → verify one observable issue → identify legitimate owner contact → personal outreach → confirm genuine interest → small unofficial homepage concept when justified → written scope → verified deposit → build/test → approval/balance → authorized launch/handoff.

The established general proof-first model permits small relevant proof before outreach, but the cash sprint should avoid days of speculative unpaid work. A targeted concept is illustrative until approved and launched; do not impersonate an official live business site.

## Cash implication
One $250 deposit is below the $500 target. Two deposits total $500 gross but create two delivery obligations. One fully paid $500 site meets the gross target; fees, holds, refunds, direct costs and settlement determine accessible money. Do not count unpaid invoices or pending settlement as available cash.

## After the sprint
Review conversion, build hours, revisions, payment delays, costs, net available cash and follow-up commitments. Keep, revise or stop the tactic based on evidence and Donovin's decision. No smaller repair offer or permanent price change is silently adopted.
