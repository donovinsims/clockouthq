# Cash, goals and measurement
As of 2026-10-29. Sources S01/S04/L01. Tracking method below is proposed; no financial records were reviewed.

## Active target
At least **$500 in available funds by November 10, 2026**. The proposed introductory offer is $500, split into $250 upfront and $250 after approval before launch (U02 scope; price revised on 2026-10-31).

Use this conservative operational definition until Donovin specifies otherwise:
Available sprint funds = cleared, accessible customer receipts minus payment fees, refunds and direct sprint costs already paid or necessarily reserved. Keep tax obligations and other required reserves visible without inventing a tax rate.

Record gross receipts, cleared receipts and net available funds separately. Do not confuse:
pipeline → quote → contract value → invoice → payment pending → cleared receipt → spendable cash.

## Minimum private cash record
Date, customer alias, project, invoice amount, deposit/balance, payment status, expected clearance, actual clearance, gross, fees, refunds, direct costs, obligations/reserves and available amount. Store detailed client financial records privately; put only a sourced aggregate in CURRENT_STATE.

Gap = max(0, $500 − verified available sprint funds). Do not calculate from blank tracker cells as if blank meant zero.

## Decision-useful metrics
Prospects researched, qualified, contacted, replies, conversations, quotes, agreements, deposits, completed sites, balances and cleared funds. Every rate needs a denominator and date range.

Delivery: hours, rework, waiting time, missed deadline, defects and support burden. Economics: receipt minus direct costs, with founder time visible. Do not label gross receipts as profit.

## Review dates
Daily during the sprint: cash/settlement and next action. November 10: actual target outcome and remaining obligations. Afterward: weekly pipeline/delivery and monthly economics review if useful.

## ROI discipline
Estimate baseline and post-change volume/time/errors. State assumptions and attribution limits. Reclaimed owner hours are capacity; they are cash savings only when spending actually falls or measured contribution increases. Avoid performance guarantees.

## Monthly financial review procedure

On each review date, reconcile the private cash record to statements or the authorized payment source: opening available cash, cleared receipts, fees, refunds, direct costs, taxes/obligations and closing available cash. Reconcile invoices separately; unpaid or pending amounts are not available cash. For each project calculate collected revenue minus direct costs, then show founder hours, rework and support separately. Compare acquisition stages using the same date window and denominator. Record one decision—continue, change, pause or stop—plus evidence, owner and next review date. Unknown balances, entity, insurance, banking and tax status remain open decisions requiring appropriate professional advice.
