## Why the Ghostwriter integration is selective

The upstream skills generally classify a large set of terms as banned AI language. **That rule is not suitable for authentic-Corey matching** because words such as "optimize", "framework" and "roadmap" are attested in the supplied sample. The adapted skills retain the useful feature extraction and final editing process, while using the real corpus over generic bans. Only Voice Analyze and Voice Apply are incorporated; Voice Create and Voice Blend are intentionally excluded.

## Evidence and limits

The measured corpus is 405 deduplicated originals (395 in profile measurements, 10 held out). Format counts: 44 short, 222 long, 107 quote, 10 thread roots and 12 articles. The source material is not authorization to publish as Corey. Business outcomes in the bio are attributed historical self-reports, and the ICP includes labeled hypotheses. The new tone sliders are *editorial estimates*, not research-measured probabilities. No blinded human evaluation or causal engagement test was run.

See `TESTING_AND_LIMITATIONS.md` and `CHANGELOG.md`.
