# Donovin Sims | Two-audience ICP

Initial strategic hypotheses from the owner's brief and Clockout records, not a measured audience survey. Shared message: AI should make real work and everyday life easier, more capable, and more effective.

## Audience A: Working professionals and everyday AI learners

| Dimension | Guidance |
|---|---|
| Demographics / professional stage | Adults across career stages, nine-to-five employees, nontechnical professionals and everyday learners. No invented age, income, degree or location threshold. |
| Psychographics | Curious but overwhelmed by tool churn; frustrated by repetitive tasks; wants competence and control without technical gatekeeping. |
| Motivation | Do useful work with less friction, learn transferable skills, become more capable in work and daily life. |
| Goals | Understand inputs and outputs, reuse a workflow, build a bounded tool or assistant, evaluate output confidently. |
| Pain points | Blank-page prompting, confusing jargon, unreliable outputs, disconnected tools, no clear starting task, fear of breaking something. |
| Objections | “I'm not technical.” “I don't have time.” “How do I know it's right?” “Can I use work data?” Address with a small demonstration, test criteria and approved/sanitized inputs. |
| Decision behavior | Tries a low-effort example, checks the result, then expands when useful. Learning does not imply buying consulting. |
| Content preferences | Beginner walkthroughs, before/after process diagrams, reusable prompts, examples with expected outputs, failure modes and simple agent builds. |
| Language | Plain English, explain a technical term once, one task at a time, concrete inputs and outputs. Avoid income pitches and superiority. |
| CTAs | Try on a safe sample; compare to the source; save a checklist; adapt one step. No invented downloads or compulsory sales funnel. |
| Anti-ICP | People seeking guaranteed AI income, agency-startup coaching, instant expertise or instructions that bypass workplace permissions. |
| Success metrics | Useful reader questions, reported task completion, repeat use, saves, corrections and demonstrated skill growth. Engagement is a proxy; do not invent learning results. |

## Audience B: Business owners, founders and operators

| Dimension | Guidance |
|---|---|
| Demographics / business stage | Owner-led small and established service businesses, busy founders and operators with real workflow complexity. Initial Clockout focus: Rockford/Stateline; personal readership can be broader. |
| Psychographics | Skeptical of hype, protective of customer trust, time-poor, wants an accountable person who understands the work. |
| Motivation | Reduce repetitive administration, improve follow-up, connect systems, reduce owner dependence and improve reliable handoffs. |
| Goals | Diagnose one expensive bottleneck, implement a feasible fix, get clear ownership, adoption and acceptance checks. |
| Pain points | Missed inquiries, duplicate entry, scattered information, manual scheduling, unowned tasks and disconnected systems. |
| Objections | Cost, disruption, trust, maintenance, data access, staff adoption and previous tool disappointments. Show a bounded proposal, exception path and testable acceptance criteria. |
| Decision behavior | Evaluates a practical demonstration, business relevance, purchasing ability, access, support capacity and implementation feasibility before engaging. |
| Qualification | Demonstrated operational pain, meaningful impact, an accountable decision-maker, ability to pay, suitable permissions and a manageable solution. No universal revenue/employee minimum copied from Corey. |
| Content preferences | Diagnostic checklists, transparent sample calculations, process maps, when automation is unsuitable, verified cases with appropriate permission. |
| Language | Leads, time, cost, customer response, handoffs, responsibilities and next steps. Explain the process before the stack. |
| CTAs | Inspect a handoff or map the workflow; where appropriate discuss the bounded Free Business Look. Paid assessment only when warranted, then implementation; recurring work only when justified. |
| Anti-ICP | No owner, no meaningful problem, no access/permission, no purchasing ability, unrealistic guaranteed ROI, or support obligations Clockout cannot reliably deliver. |
| Success metrics | Qualified conversations, relevant diagnostic requests, agreed scope, accepted implementation, adoption and measured process outcomes. Count actual records only. |

## Content and offer map

60% practical education primarily serves A. 20% tools/agents/builds can serve A or B. 15% operations primarily serves B. 5% authentic journey builds context for both. Allocate each idea once by primary purpose. See `../content-strategy.json` for configurable planning weights and `../validation/20-post-plan.json` for the 12/4/3/1 example.

A learns to do it themselves; B may hire diagnosis and implementation. Do not collapse both into a consulting-sales audience. Clockout's business identity and personal educational identity reinforce each other while retaining different motivations and CTAs.

## Unknowns to validate

Actual audience composition, preferred formats, reading level, conversion rates, willingness to pay and geographic reach remain unknown. Update from real questions, owner-approved feedback and observed results. No age bands, follower count, revenue history or adoption evidence has been inferred.
