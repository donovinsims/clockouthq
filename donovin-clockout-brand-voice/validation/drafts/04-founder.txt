I grew up in Roscoe, Illinois, and played Division I baseball at Northern Illinois University.

During college summers I worked for the Winnebago County Highway Department. Later came product and operations work at Uber, then digital product work at Walgreens.

Now I'm building Clockout as a solo founder.

Those are different environments. The thread I want to carry into this business is practical: understand the work, make the handoff clear, and build something people can actually use.

That's also how I want to teach AI.

Start with a task someone recognizes. Show the steps. Explain how to check the result. Give them enough understanding to try it themselves.

I don't need a dramatic origin story to explain that direction.

I want the work and the explanation to be useful.
