Stop asking AI to “clean up” your meeting notes.

Give it a finish line:

“Turn these sample notes into a table with four columns: task, owner, deadline, and source sentence. If an owner or date is missing, write unknown. Don't fill the gaps.”

Then check the table against the notes.

1) Does every task point back to something someone actually said?
2) Did it turn a suggestion into a commitment?
3) Are the missing details still visible?

Use a made-up example first. For real work, use your organization's approved tools and data rules.

The useful part is the review step. A tidy action list can still be wrong.

Once the small example works, save the instructions and reuse them. You now have a workflow you can inspect instead of a vague request you have to rewrite.
