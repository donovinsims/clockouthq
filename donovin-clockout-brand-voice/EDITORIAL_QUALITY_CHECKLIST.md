- [ ] One lead audience: A learns practical AI; B evaluates a business improvement.
- [ ] One primary category, counted once toward the configurable 60/20/15/5 initial target.
- [ ] One useful idea with actual mechanics, an example or a clear way to test it.
- [ ] CTA fits the audience and an existing verified destination; omit when unnecessary.

## Voice and usefulness
- [ ] Direct, practical, commercially grounded, generous, conversational and evidence-conscious.
- [ ] Concrete opening, varied sentence/paragraph lengths, clear steps and useful ending.
- [ ] No manufactured typos, forced lowercase, repeated catchphrases, generic hype or empty questions.
- [ ] Punctuation/format follows adopted mechanics without turning reference frequencies into quotas.
- [ ] Ghostwriter diagnostics remove boilerplate and artificial symmetry; optimize/framework/roadmap/leverage remain contextual vocabulary.

- [ ] Used 3-6 useful examples, with everyday and stronger/contrasting sources when relevant. Recorded actual IDs, URLs, selection reasons and transferable techniques privately.
