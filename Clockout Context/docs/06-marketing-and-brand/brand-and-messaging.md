# Brand and messaging
Sources L01/L04 and S04. Public copy below is proposed wording, not a publishing action.

## Identity and voice
Clockout, capital C only. Donovin's voice is first person, local, direct, practical and credible to skeptical owners. Prefer everyday words about calls, quotes, customers, paperwork and follow-up. Explain the outcome before technology.

Core message: **Find the bottleneck. Fix the process. Automate the rest.**
Primary established CTA: **Get a free look at your business.**

Use short sentences and clear examples. Avoid generic AI hype, jargon, fake urgency, invented statistics, robot imagery and claims of effortless revenue. Do not copy Corey's words or identity.

## Message hierarchy
1. Recognizable business problem.
2. Practical result and how Donovin works.
3. Small proof or an honest example.
4. Owner control and clear limits.
5. One next action.

For the website sprint, lead with the actual website/customer problem and a scoped replacement. Do not redefine all of Clockout as a cheap website agency.

## Proof rules
Examples and demos are labeled illustrations. Testimonials, client names, logos, before/after results and numbers require permission and substantiation. Employment history does not imply employer endorsement.

The earlier source promise of a reply within 60 minutes is recorded historically, not adopted here as a permanent SLA. Confirm actual availability before repeating it.

## Suggested general description
Clockout helps local owners find and fix bottlenecks in how their businesses run. Donovin studies the process, simplifies it, and implements practical technology where it helps. Start with a free look at one meaningful problem.

## Marketing priorities
A credible site and reliable contact path, personal targeted outreach, useful proof, customer results and referrals. Content, workshops and local partnerships can support the business where evidence warrants them. Mandatory social posting and a LinkedIn agency strategy are not established requirements.

## Design direction
Calm, minimal, monochrome light/dark, mobile-first and native iOS-inspired quality. The later October context specifies ObsidianUI first, COSS fallback. The September implementation records differ. Read the [website status](website-status.md) and OD15 before touching implementation.
## Message-use procedure

Before publishing or sending copy, identify audience, customer problem, one claim, evidence source, call to action and owner approval. Prefer the promise “Find the bottleneck. Fix the process. Automate the rest.” and the public entry offer “Get a free look at your business.” Use a specific observation or approved result as proof; label examples and hypotheses. Do not claim rankings, revenue, client results, certifications or automation outcomes without dated evidence. Review for plain language, accessibility, contact accuracy and whether the CTA has an owner and response path. Record the approved version and date so later edits do not silently change positioning.
