# Website project working forms

Copy the relevant section into a private working document for each prospect or client. Proposed examples are not evidence of real activity. Keep credentials and unnecessary personal information elsewhere.

## Prospect research and qualification
- Business / URL / location / source and date:
- Service, audience, geography (source and confidence):
- Contact, role and verification source / preferred public channel:
- Observed issue, exact page/device/date / screenshot location:
- Possible customer-task impact (hypothesis, not measured loss):
- Scores 0–2: friction / relevance / reachable owner / feasible scope / significance; total /10:
- Missing facts / disqualifier / decision: pursue, hold or decline:
- Relevant proof and permission / angle / next action and date:

## Website audit
- Page, date, viewport, browser / tester:
- Business identity and offer: pass / issue / unverified; evidence:
- Mobile and desktop layout; navigation and customer task:
- Contact, CTA and forms (no unsolicited test submissions):
- Trust and content accuracy / accessibility basics:
- Search title and indexability / HTTPS / performance measurement method:
- Priority: blocker, important or cosmetic; observed fact versus impact hypothesis:
- Proposed next step / limitations / whether site owner has authorized further testing:

## Discovery and lead qualification
- Customer's objective and words / current workflow and last example:
- What is lost or delayed, evidence and baseline / who is affected:
- Decision-maker and approver / authority to grant access:
- Budget, deadline, content readiness, alternatives and constraints:
- Existing domain, hosting, contact destination, analytics and ownership:
- Decision: fit / no fit / unanswered questions; next action and owner:

## Proposal and scope of work
- Client / date / expiry / approver:
- Problem, desired result, deliverables and explicit exclusions:
- Pages, content and contact method / accessibility and QA commitments:
- Responsibilities, client inputs and deadlines; dependencies and pause process:
- Price, fees/taxes where applicable, deposit and balance milestones:
- One revision round defined as: / change request rate or quote process:
- Acceptance criteria / review period / how defects differ from new requests:
- Domain, hosting, ongoing charges, ownership and access transfer:
- Launch permission, support window and limits / cancellation and refund terms:
- Signature or documented acceptance / no implied guarantee of traffic or sales:

## Content intake and website requirements
- Legal business name, approved display name, services and geography:
- Verified contact details and destination for form submissions:
- Approved headline, service descriptions, CTA and required disclosures:
- Logo, images, copy and proof, sources and reuse permission:
- Domain registrar, hosting, access owner and account invitation method (no passwords):
- Required device, browser and accessibility needs / page layout and exclusions:
- Missing items, client owner and date / approval of facts:

## Design review and revision
- Private preview / date / reviewer and authority:
- What supports the agreed task / what fails acceptance:
- One consolidated list: item, page, desired change, in scope Y/N, decision:
- Out-of-scope change request, impact on price/time and written approval:
- Approval to proceed to QA, or outstanding blocker:

## QA and client approval
- [ ] Approved content and contact details checked by client
- [ ] Mobile and desktop layout, navigation and every link tested
- [ ] Keyboard access, labels, focus visibility and basic text readability checked
- [ ] Form test authorized, submitted, received by correct recipient, test record handled
- [ ] Metadata, HTTPS, redirects and security reviewed; no credentials in source
- [ ] Domain/hosting ownership and renewal responsibilities documented
- [ ] Backup/rollback plan, issues, severity and owner recorded
- [ ] Written acceptance and agreed payment milestone confirmed
- [ ] Explicit launch permission recorded; launch and public re-test separately recorded
- Client approval by / date / scope / known exceptions:

## Handoff and post-project review
- Live URL and launch date / access transferred to / account owners:
- How to update content; renewal dates/cost owners; support and escalation boundaries:
- Backups and rollback location / open issues and owners:
- Approved completion record / balance status / follow-up date:
- Baseline, metric and review window / evidence / client permission for testimonial:
- Hours, direct costs, fees, rework / what to repeat, change or stop:

## Payment record
- Proposal amount / agreed deposit and balance due dates / invoice reference:
- Invoice sent / payment pending / settled / available dates and amounts:
- Fees, refunds, holds, direct costs and net available:
- Verify against payment source; never count a quote or unsettled payment as available cash.
