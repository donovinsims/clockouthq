## Completeness boundary
This is a comprehensive operating baseline built from recovered evidence, with the agreed structure and requested operational coverage. “Complete history” means all material recovered events are integrated and gaps are visible; it cannot honestly mean that inaccessible events have been recreated.

No assumptions fill the missing history. Unknown revenue and client counts stay unknown. A reported previous ZIP integrity check is not repeated here as a verified fact.

## Deliberate exclusions
Private current-client details, actual customer payment records, home address, credentials, banking information, unrelated personal life, raw session state and complete third-party template/source collections. Historical Pietro's work is summarized only at project level and is not active backlog.

Third-party transcripts are cataloged for on-demand use in the existing library. They are not redistributed wholesale in this ZIP. This preserves portability without turning the business knowledge base into an unreviewed archive dump.
