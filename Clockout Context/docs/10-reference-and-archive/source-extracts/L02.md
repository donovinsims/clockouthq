I'm Donovin. I grew up in Roscoe, Illinois. Went to Hononegah High School, played three sports, then played Division I baseball at Northern Illinois University.

Every summer during college I came home and worked for the Winnebago County Highway Department. That job stuck with me. You showed up early. You did the work. I watched men and women get out of bed at 5 a.m. every morning, put in a full day, then do it again tomorrow because people were counting on them. That's what tough looks like.

After NIU I went into tech and became an Operations Manager at Uber.

At Uber I built rideshare operations for some of the largest live events in North America. Cities, music venues, sports arenas, crowds of 70,000 people who all needed to get home at once. I helped drive $7M+ in revenue by building better infrastructure and redesigning the rider experience for partners like the Super Bowl, the NBA, and MLB.

When that many people need to leave at the same time, small problems become big ones fast. Dispatch has to work. Handoffs have to be clean. Everyone needs to know exactly what they're doing. That's the standard I still work by.

A few years later I joined Walgreens Corporate, shipping digital products at national retail scale. Today I bring that same rigor to you and your business. I diagnose the workflow costing you the most time, speed, or trust, then build a governed, tested solution around it. Not a generic AI tool bolted onto a broken process.

Then ChatGPT came out and I got hooked instantly.

Since then I've spent thousands of hours and thousands of dollars testing AI tools, building workflows, breaking them, rebuilding them. Trying to figure out which ones actually save time, make money, and move the needle for a real business. Most don't. And the ones that do, most people never use to their potential.

Over time I landed on a simple way of working. Audit. Optimize. Automate.

Find where time and money are leaking. Fix the process. Automate the parts that don't need a human in the loop. That's the work I do now.

I help non-technical operators and business owners who know they should probably be using AI but aren't sure where to start. I care about useful AI. The kind that saves you a few hours a week, takes a repetitive task off your plate, helps you make a better decision, or fixes a process that's been annoying everyone for years.

My goal is simple: teach you to fish, not sell you the shortcut.
