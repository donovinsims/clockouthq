## 1. Why this folder was reorganized

This workspace mixes four kinds of material: the Clockout business context, the Corey-Ganim mentor system, the raw Corey source archive, and a library of agent templates. Before cleanup it also contained macOS junk, byte-for-byte duplicates, two copies of most podcast episodes (in two formats), and a complete context kit for a **dead brand** ("Return My Time") that could contaminate any AI agent loaded into the folder. The goal: one copy of everything, a structure any agent can navigate, and zero chance of loading the wrong brand.
