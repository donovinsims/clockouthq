# Attio synchronization specification

Status as of November 8, 2026: architecture approved under U10 / D24. W06 verifies schema only. [OD13](../OPEN_DECISIONS.md#od13--p2) tracks readiness. The [Operations Manager spec](operations-manager-spec.md) owns the local runtime. No CRM records were inspected or changed for this schema review.

## Ownership and observed schema

Local SQLite and owner-private artifact files contain complete Clockout-managed prospect and operational records, including raw evidence, screenshots, findings, briefs, task states, approvals, run events, execution logs, and the history of CRM projections and reconciliation. Research and local workflows must work offline from Attio. Attio is the existing CRM for Companies, People, Deals and sales follow-up. Controlled local-to-Attio projections provide sales context. Attio-owned sales edits do not drive local state transitions or approvals. When relevant, an explicit operator reconciliation records CRM observations and decisions locally. There is no automatic inbound write-back in the initial design.

Read-only metadata on November 8 confirmed the standard `companies`, `people`, and `deals` objects and the Companies-backed `clockout_pipeline` list. Companies exposes writable `name`, unique `domains`, `primary_location`, `categories`, and `description`. People exposes writable `name`, unique `email_addresses`, `company`, `job_title`, and `phone_numbers`. Deals requires `name`, `stage`, and `owner` and supports `associated_company`, `associated_people`, and `value`. Its observed stages are Lead, In Progress, Won, and Lost. The list has writable `ledger_id`, `phone`, `contact_status`, `next_action`, and `follow_up_date`. The observed `ledger_id` is text without a uniqueness constraint. No dedicated pipeline list stage, website URL, qualification score, audit finding, or demo URL field was found in the inspected schema. Recheck field types, option IDs, workspace and permissions before enabling sync. No schema additions are required for the pilot.

| Local information | Initial Attio target | Ownership and write rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Company identity, website and location | Company `name`, `domains`, `primary_location`; full verified website URL in the company summary | Local evidence proposes facts. Seed verified values on a new record. On a matched record preserve current CRM values, compare and flag conflicts. Never replace domain collections. Keep full URL and path in the note. |
| Industry | Company summary; `categories` only when an existing option is explicitly mapped and authorized for a new record | Local owns the evidence. Reuse an exact option when it fits. Do not invent an option or overwrite CRM enrichment or categories. |
| Verified contacts and business phone | People `name`, `email_addresses`, `company`, `job_title`, `phone_numbers`; list `phone` when safely seeded | Sync only verified business contacts with a permissible purpose. Attach People to the matched Company on authorized creation. Preserve existing CRM contact values and relationships; flag disagreement. A shared inbox is a company contact route, not a Person identity. |
| Stable identity and list membership | Local prospect ID linked to the Attio Company and Pipeline entry IDs. Candidate use of list `ledger_id` | Keep the ID crosswalk in SQLite. Confirm existing `ledger_id` semantics before using it. Never overwrite an existing value, assume uniqueness, or adopt its values as local IDs without reconciliation. Leave the field alone until verified. |
| Qualification, key findings and sources | Versioned note on the linked Company | Local owns score and evidence. Sync a short dated summary with key findings, confidence and permitted public source URLs. Keep raw artifacts and internal records local. |
| Prospect stage, demo URL and outreach outcome | Company summary; list `contact_status` only after its intended use and single-writer ownership are confirmed | Local owns workflow and verified contact outcomes. Include a demo URL only when the exact published artifact is verified and authorized for CRM sharing. Keep access tokens, private links and outreach payloads local. Never infer contact from a draft or CRM stage. |
| Suggested next action and CRM follow-up | Local suggested action in the summary; existing list `next_action` and `follow_up_date` are CRM-owned | An approved initial seed may populate an empty field at entry creation. Leave later staff edits intact. Do not create CRM tasks that could trigger messages. |
| Deal, owner, stage, value and associated records | Existing Deal, explicitly linked to its Company and People where applicable | Attio owns the sales lifecycle. Link an existing Deal only after explicit match; the pilot keeps new Deal creation and stage changes founder-controlled. Do not manufacture a deal, amount, owner, or progress from research or approval state. |

Use the note for fields without a safe native mapping and for proposed changes to existing CRM fields. Publish at most one concise note per meaningful local projection revision. Use a stable, non-sensitive marker to reconcile it. Keep notes distinct from manual CRM notes. Store the most recent verified CRM values and any resolved conflicts in SQLite alongside the projection receipt. Never treat an Attio edit as permission to build, publish a preview, contact someone, or deploy production.

## Matching, outbox and reconciliation

1. **Resolve before writing.** Match an existing stored workspace/object/record ID first and recheck its identity. Otherwise use an exact verified normalized domain for a Company or verified individual email for a Person. Shared domains, branches and ambiguous or missing matches require owner review. Company name alone is insufficient. Create a new record only under the approved sync policy with a verified unique key and no match. On a uniqueness conflict, read and reconcile. Never use a blind upsert to replace human-owned values. Resolve Pipeline entries by list and parent Company, persist the entry ID, and hold if multiple entries match. Never merge or delete automatically. [Attio record upsert](https://docs.attio.com/rest-api/endpoint-reference/records/upsert-a-record) · [Attio list-entry upsert](https://docs.attio.com/rest-api/endpoint-reference/entries/upsert-a-list-entry-by-parent)
2. **Queue after local validation.** Persist a bounded allowlisted projection and its outbox job in the same SQLite transaction as the qualifying local change. Use a unique key for the workspace, local target, operation and projection digest. Track local ID, remote IDs, revision, attempt count, timestamps, next retry, remote outcome, and redacted diagnostics. Create or bind Company first, then People/list entry and note. Resume after the last verified step. Leave raw screenshots, briefs, private contact material, approval and execution events, payment data and credentials outside the payload.
3. **Run independently.** A separately authorized sync runner automatically drains the local outbox in bounded batches. Use the existing authorized connector where it supports the verified runtime and operations. Use Attio's official API with approved minimum scopes only if needed and separately authorized. Keep credentials outside Git, the research and build identities, and renderer inputs. The adapter has no mail, preview, production, schema-write or approval capability. Verify existing CRM automations will not turn list changes, contact fields or notes into outreach or other unapproved actions. Keep the runner disabled if the side effects cannot be bounded.
4. **Handle conflict conservatively.** Compare relevant remote values with last confirmed values and the proposed projection. Record mismatches without automatic overwrite. For existing records, default to linking and managed notes. Allow direct updates only to explicitly designated integration-owned fields with verified exclusive write control or a reliable conditional update. A read followed by an unconditional write does not guarantee conflict safety. Where neither protection is available, retain the existing value and record the proposal in the managed note or hold it for operator review.
5. **Reconcile before retrying ambiguous writes.** Record confirmed outcomes, pending work, rate limits, permanent failures and conflicts separately. Honor `Retry-After` on rate limits. Use bounded backoff for retryable operations. After timeouts or partial writes, check the verified unique key, parent/entry match, or note marker and confirm the actual outcome before retrying. If a creation cannot be safely reconciled, hold it for operator review. Stop on auth failures, schema drift, or unresolved conflicts. Pause the runner without blocking research or local work. [Attio rate limiting](https://docs.attio.com/rest-api/guides/rate-limiting) · [Attio notes](https://docs.attio.com/rest-api/endpoint-reference/notes/list-notes)

## Readiness gates

Phase 2 proves local persistence, restore, outbox and conflict behavior with a disabled or inert adapter. Test offline operation, missing or ambiguous identities, repeated projection, partial writes, ambiguous notes, rate limit handling and remote edits against fixtures. Phase 3 research remains local. Before the Phase 5 pilot can enable live sync, recheck the workspace metadata, existing mappings and CRM automations, verify the least-privilege execution path, and obtain separate authorization for a scoped synthetic or founder-approved sync test. Demonstrate no duplicates, no unintended overwrites or notifications, correct payload minimization, and independent local operation during a provider outage. Confirm retry and reconciliation logs and a manual pause/recovery path. Phase 6 can continue the verified automatic runner independently of scheduled research. No new object, list, field or unrestricted inbound sync is part of this approval.
