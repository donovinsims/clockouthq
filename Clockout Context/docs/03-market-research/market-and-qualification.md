# Market and prospect qualification
Sources: L01, S04, S12. Market strategy established; scoring below is a PROPOSED operating aid.

## Initial geography
Roscoe, Rockford, Rockton, Loves Park, Machesney Park, Belvidere and South Beloit, with adjacent northern Illinois/Stateline communities when practical.

Potential categories include home services, trades, automotive, cleaning, landscaping, restoration, appointment-driven businesses and high-value independent operators. These are research pools, not permanent niche commitments.

## Evidence-led qualification
For each prospect record:
- Business, town, public source URL and observation date.
- Accessible owner/decision-maker and appropriate contact channel.
- One directly observed problem; separate plausible inference.
- Why solving it could matter economically.
- Existing website/tools and likely implementation constraints.
- The smallest appropriate proof, or a no-pitch reason.
- Next action, owner and date.

An outdated design is not enough by itself. Determine whether a customer can understand the service, contact the owner and take the next step on a phone. A strong website does not disqualify a business from operational improvement.

## Proposed quick screen
Rate evidence quality, problem significance, owner access, budget/urgency and feasibility as high / medium / low / unknown. Use these judgments as the general default. The website manual also offers a proposed 0–2 sorting aid; it is not a calibrated sales predictor.

Advance when there is a specific observed issue, a credible potential benefit, reachable decision-maker, and a feasible small engagement. Investigate unknowns in conversation. Reject when no worthwhile problem exists, the prospect wants unsupported guarantees, or access/delivery cannot work.

## Sprint filter
Through November 10, favor a simple website need that can be sold and delivered in time for funds to settle. Prefer owner-ready content, clear scope and uncomplicated hosting. Avoid complex commerce, integrations or speculative builds at the temporary price unless separately scoped.

## Research limits
Public observation cannot establish private lead leakage, internal costs, software adoption or willingness to pay. Do not pose as a customer, send fake inquiries, or invent customer interviews. Archived June lead CSVs are historical lead inventories; they were not validated for this sprint and are not included as active prospects.

## Stop criteria and missing-site verification
Reject or pause when there is no material issue, ownership/permission is unclear, no practical budget/timing fit, sensitive scope exceeds competence, access is infeasible, or the owner has already solved the issue. A missing GBP website field is not proof a business has no website: check its own public pages, search result and linked social/contact information. Do not send fake customer inquiries to manufacture a response-time problem.

Use high/medium/low/unknown for general opportunities. For website batches, the website operating system offers an optional numeric sorting aid with evidence requirements and disqualifier overrides. Neither method substitutes for owner validation. Source A02/U02.
