## First work block

## Rolling daily loop
1. Check customer delivery obligations and payment timing.
2. Research a manageable batch; qualify on evidence, owner access, fit and feasibility.
3. Contact owners personally with one observation and a small invitation.
4. Hold useful conversations and send clear scopes promptly.
5. Verify deposits; build only accepted paid scope.
6. Test, obtain approval, collect balance before launch, and hand off.
7. Record results and the next action/date.

Batch sizes should fit real delivery capacity. No fixed conversion rate or outreach quota has evidence yet.

## If behind
