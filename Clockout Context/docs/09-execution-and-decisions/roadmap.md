## Select the next work block

This roadmap records a dated sequence, not evidence that scheduled work occurred. Before using it, check CURRENT_STATE's business baseline and any newer founder decision. List accepted customer obligations and payment deadlines first; then choose one sales or delivery task with an output that can be verified in the available block. Each backlog item needs next action, owner, dependency, evidence of done and review trigger. A tool experiment advances only when it solves a current dependency; otherwise park it.

At a review, compare actual progress with the objective. Carry incomplete obligations forward explicitly, close obsolete ideas with a reason, and choose one primary objective. For market selection use the business plan's evidence gates; for the sprint-to-property-management decision use the November 10 review. Passing a date never completes work or authorizes a new phase.
