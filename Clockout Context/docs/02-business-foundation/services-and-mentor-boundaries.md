## Corey Ganim's role
Corey is an external mentor and educational reference. Donovin wants the complete methodology, including advanced approaches, rather than only beginner material. Adopt principles thoughtfully; do not copy identity, wording, branding, prices or achievements.

Useful principles include diagnosis before technology, measurable outcomes, recurring operational pain, paid assessments when warranted, clear done-for-you versus done-with-you obligations, repeatable delivery and justified recurring revenue.

Reported reference prices include about $999 for assessments, $1,000–$2,500/month for Concierge, about $2,500 for workshops and $6,500–$7,500/month for fractional leadership examples (S09). They are neither a Clockout rate card nor current verified market prices. Do not quote them to a Clockout customer as adopted terms.

## Two different 90-day plans
Clockout's company-building sequence: selling assets and conversations → validated diagnosis/implementation → measurement and selective recurring services.

Corey's client transformation sequence: administration → sales → delivery/fulfillment.

Neither sequence proves completed work. The current cash sprint temporarily takes precedence over both.

## Advanced opportunities gate
Before launching any advanced service, establish a real buyer/problem, exact deliverables, required access, economics, acceptance criteria, risk ownership, support capacity and explicit owner adoption. Add it to the active offer register only after that decision.
