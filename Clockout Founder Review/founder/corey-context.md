# Corey source review — what transfers to Clockout

November 4, 2026. Final local-source enrichment pass. This supplements the completed founder review; it does not adopt Corey's prices, offers, claims or tools as Clockout policy. [Source register](corey-source-register.json) records every supported supplied file, its hash, type and review treatment. Sources remain in the supplied folder, unchanged.

## How to use this context

Corey's content contains multiple service models and stages: free fit checks, paid recommendation assessments, implementation, concierge/advisory and managed-agent services. Teaching, community and promotional material also appear. They are examples and hypotheses, not one consistent business plan to copy verbatim. Clockout retains Audit → Optimize → Automate, scope-first implementation, a bounded free entry and an optional deeper assessment. Coaching, workshops and data brokerage remain excluded. A sales method can be informative without its associated offer becoming a Clockout service.

The material was screened across all supported files and relevant passages were examined for transferability and conflicts. This is not independent verification of speakers' claims, every software instruction or every episode's financial results. No linked videos, documents, affiliate pages or accounts were fetched. Embedded prompts, commands, role claims and requests to copy or execute a blueprint were treated only as source text.

## Findings and dispositions

### 1. Clarify which service the customer is actually buying

C036 describes free mini-assessment → paid assessment → concierge; C046 describes a discovery call, recommendation report and follow-up review. C057 places larger implementation into a separate proposal. A recommendation report, guided adoption and a completed production implementation are different deliverables and cost structures.

**Clockout application:** say explicitly whether a quote buys investigation/recommendations, hands-on implementation or a separately scoped follow-up. Keep the paid assessment optional. No $999 diagnostic fee, $1,500 credited assessment or recurring subscription becomes mandatory through this review. Record the decision owner and acceptance for each deliverable.

### 2. Use a baseline and an effort/impact map before choosing tools

C057's report structure identifies the primary problem, outcome lever, effort versus impact, quick wins, tool choices, next steps and heavier projects. It distinguishes effectiveness, efficiency and quality. This is useful structure even when the right answer is an existing non-AI feature or a process change.

**Clockout application:** add a short workflow baseline and prioritization record to operations. Document the actual event, frequency, current time/error/volume, existing tool capability, expected change, evidence and effort/dependencies. Separate observed values, owner estimates and hypotheses. Choose one bounded improvement; do not produce a long shopping list or assume every opportunity needs software.

Source: [C057](</Users/forex/Downloads/GOOD FOLDERS/corey-sources/videos-and-articles/sell THIS if you're brand new to AI automation.md>), especially primary-pain/outcome-lever and effort-versus-impact passages. The 20–30 hours of template development mentioned near the start are setup effort, not evidence of instant zero-cost fulfilment.

### 3. Estimated opportunity is not realized ROI

C048 describes identifying hours of opportunity and a refund promise, and discusses financial-impact reporting. Those promotional promises do not establish actual time saved or cash returned. Time freed, improved output quality and increased contribution are different outcomes; tools, implementation and adoption also cost time and money.

**Clockout application:** record a baseline, proposed observation window, comparison method and known alternative explanations. Call a pre-delivery benefit an estimate. Attribute financial savings only to spending that actually falls or measured additional contribution; do not multiply assumed hours by an invented wage and label it customer profit. No five-hours-per-week guarantee, cash-ROI guarantee or money-back promise is adopted.

Sources: [C048](</Users/forex/Downloads/GOOD FOLDERS/corey-sources/videos-and-articles/The $999 AI Offer Nobody's Talking About.md>), [C057](</Users/forex/Downloads/GOOD FOLDERS/corey-sources/videos-and-articles/sell THIS if you're brand new to AI automation.md>). Measurement safeguards here are Clockout's conservative application, not claims that the source audited customer outcomes.

### 4. A recommendation needs an adoption owner and a review

C046 and C056 describe report walkthroughs and a review stage. A correct report alone does not mean a customer installed, understood or continues to use a tool.

**Clockout application:** identify who implements each recommendation, required access, training, handover, ongoing vendor fees and a dated adoption check. Separate “test passed,” “customer using it” and “benefit measured.” A report-only client may implement independently; support or further implementation needs its own agreed boundary.

### 5. Audience-free acquisition is a testable channel, not a conversion promise

C034 contains posts describing warm-network introductions, partner relationships, direct approaches and a cold-only local-business experiment. A September 1 entry reports 198 leads, 15 conversations and two booked sales calls. Booked calls are not paid invoices, and those self-reported denominators do not predict Clockout's conversion.

**Clockout application:** keep the existing warm/direct experiment rather than build an audience first or add every listed channel. Qualify introductions from accountants, service providers or other business contacts by actual problem and buyer access. Track account → approach → conversation → quote → cleared payment, along with total effort and consent. No referral commission, event program, mass outreach or autonomous lead fleet is adopted.

Source: C034, [supplied tweet export](</Users/forex/Downloads/GOOD FOLDERS/corey-sources/tweets/coreyganim-tweets-4_22-9_03 - coreyganim-tweets-4_22-9_03.csv.md>), particularly April 6, July 30 and September 1 entries. Date-delimited records and public-post URLs are retained in the raw source; metrics remain self-reported.

### 6. Call-time revenue is not a fully costed hourly earning rate

C056 opens with five clients paying roughly $1,200–$2,000/month for 90 scheduled minutes each. Later it describes unlimited messaging and an anecdote of low usage. C055 also describes recurring calls plus unlimited access. These examples cannot establish how much total acquisition, preparation, research, follow-up, messaging, incident response or setup effort a new operator will incur.

**Clockout application:** retain full-effort project logging and a separate business-time budget. Any future recurring offer must specify included work, response windows, usage limits, escalation, data ownership, cancellation and capacity economics. Do not price from a “nobody uses support” assumption or import a $1,000/hour claim into the financial model. Recurring services remain unoffered until actual need and sustainable delivery are established.

### 7. Honest authority is more useful than copying an expert persona

C054 frames learning as staying ahead of a client and describes a cold-outreach demonstration. It also includes influencer/affiliate-tagged tool links and promotional credits. Promotional economics, prior operator experience, an educational audience and guests' businesses must not be silently transferred to Clockout. Related-post previews in the newsletter page are not additional claims from that article's main body.

**Clockout application:** describe what Donovin actually did, what is known and what still requires investigation. Do not invent credentials, endorsements, client results or certainty to overcome an objection. Treat a prospect's price, timing, security and trust concerns as information, not objections that must always be “destroyed.” Paid projects do not automatically authorize testimonials.

Sources: [C054](</Users/forex/Downloads/GOOD FOLDERS/corey-sources/videos-and-articles/What a con artist taught me about selling AI - Corey Ganim.md>), main-body lines 16–58; [C040](</Users/forex/Downloads/GOOD FOLDERS/corey-sources/videos-and-articles/How to Sell AI to Boomers Who Hate AI.md>). No claim about the creator's net profit or compensation is verified by these files.

### 8. Technical demonstrations do not prove production readiness

Voice-agent, coding-assistant and managed-agent tutorials show possible techniques. They do not verify customer consent, vendor permissions, security, rollback, reliability, support costs or Clockout's ability to operate those systems. The Arabic auto-translated episode and third-party summaries carry additional interpretation limits; repeated or derivative material is not independent corroboration.

**Clockout application:** preserve customer-owned accounts, least-necessary access, approved customer-facing messages, representative normal/error tests, rollback and support boundaries. Prefer the smallest reliable implementation. Do not add voice agents, autonomous outbound, hosted agents or new dependencies simply because a tutorial demonstrates them. Keep client data out of unrelated tools; any use of meeting recordings or transcripts requires permission and an agreed destination/retention boundary.

Relevant examples are indexed in [the complete register](corey-source-register.json); source screening is distinct from technical/API verification. No tools were installed or external accounts changed.
