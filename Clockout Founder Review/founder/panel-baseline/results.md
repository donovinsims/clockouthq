## What would flip a no

- A couple of owners I know in the area telling me straight that he looked at their setup and told them to change a process instead of selling them something - one name I trust, and I would take the meeting and probably decide on the spot.
- A one-page sample of what you would actually write up for a business like mine, with the price and the finish line on it, sent to my phone - no meeting first. And someone in McHenry or Winnebago County whose quote-to-job time actually dropped who will take my call.
- A name, a local number, and a guy who will come stand in my shop and show me exactly what he would change before I pay a dime.
- A reference from an owner in the trades around here who let him touch their phone and quoting, plus he shows me the fix first and I approve every word that goes to a customer before it goes live.
- Two or three owners I know, a plumber and an electrician within an hour of Roscoe, telling me straight that he fixed a real leak in their follow-up and did not touch their accounts or upsell them. Give me their numbers and I will call them myself.
- A flat number in writing up front and one of my own vendors or landlords telling me it paid for itself in the first 60 days.
- Someone I actually know, a plumber or electrician around here, telling me Donovin fixed their intake and it stuck. Then I would take the free look, not before.
- Proof from a business like mine, a truck or a one-chair shop, not a big company, that says plainly how many jobs they got out of it and what they paid, and that I can call that person myself.
- Two owners of businesses my size, nearby, who will take my call and tell me what he actually fixed and what it cost them.
- If a manager I actually know in the area used him and could name the exact thing he fixed and what it cost, I would spend the free hour. Referral from someone who is not him is the only thing that moves me.
- Two owners I actually know in the trade, one of them with a shop my size, telling me he walked their quote-and-schedule mess and it stuck without breaking anything. Then I would take the free look.
- Someone in my line of work, another trades or dental office owner within an hour of Roscoe, telling me he found a real number, like ten hours a week or $4k in quotes that went cold, and that Donovin stuck to the number he quoted. Referral beats a pitch.
