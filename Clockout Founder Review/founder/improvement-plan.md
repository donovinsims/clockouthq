## Sequence, parallel work and decision gates

1. **Local preparation:** customer-evidence capture and offer/operations templates may run in parallel. CFO reconstruction may independently record known fees and missing inputs. Marketing may prepare an empty tracker and message structures, but must wait to finalize targeting and claims. With two agent slots, use at most one worker alongside the coordinator, owning separate files. The coordinator alone updates shared evidence, checkpoint, verification and compiled outputs. Delegation is optional, not required to create this plan.
2. **One consolidated evidence request:** ask only for missing details: how each client was acquired; total hours or defensible recalled ranges by activity; direct costs and continuing support; what was accepted; payment/settlement records; reference/publication permission; current cash and obligations; and the minimum owner income needed. Prefill the known fees, calendar durations, deliverables and availability. Unknowns do not block drafting, but block claims of actual profit, sustainable pricing or launch readiness.
3. **Select the first test:** compare the two project patterns using buyer access, observed urgency, deliverability, scope risk and likely economics. Start with existing-software implementation as a hypothesis, not an adopted niche. Choose one bounded intervention for one reachable segment only when evidence supports it. If neither pattern qualifies, gather problem evidence before adding another offer. No automatic commitment to restaurants, salons or custom-platform builds.
4. **Reconcile before quoting:** offer/operations consume the research; CFO checks the resulting scope, costs, total effort and cash timing; marketing then uses the reconciled offer. Donovin adopts prices and commercial terms before any quote is sent. Do not automatically publish the $950/$2,500 ladder or add a retainer.
5. **Proposed real-world test:** over 30 days from the first authorized approach, aim for 10 completed problem conversations, three qualified written quotes and at least one cleared paid commitment for the selected intervention. These are experiment targets, not forecasts or universal conversion benchmarks. Reach a repeatability checkpoint after three paid engagements for substantially the same intervention, recording elapsed time and full delivery economics; do not force those completions into the first 30 days.
6. **Review and adapt:** review after 10 completed conversations or at day 30, whichever comes first. Insufficient conversations calls for an access/message/channel review; conversations without urgent problems call for a problem/segment review; qualified quotes without deposits call for a scope, trust, timing and price review based on actual reasons. Do not diagnose the cause from a small aggregate alone. Scope overruns or unacceptable total-hour economics require repair before repeating the sale.

## Measurement, reconciliation and verification

- Track counts and denominators: approached → conversation → qualified problem → quote → agreement → cleared payment → accepted delivery → permitted reference. Separate channels and scopes. The initial primary measure is paid commitments for the selected intervention; pair it with total hours, cash contribution, unresolved support and acceptance status.
- Where inputs exist, cash contribution equals collections minus project cash costs; contribution per total hour divides that figure by all project-related labor. Neither includes allocated fixed overhead, founder wage or tax unless explicitly shown. A sustainability scenario must also account for overhead, non-project business time and the owner's income requirement. Cash, project profitability and owner take-home are separate measures.
- Treat the existing 18-hour Core, 8-hour One Fix, capacity cap and annual model as scenarios until matching evidence supports changes. Do not put the two historical project fees into the annual ramp or infer full-time throughput from availability alone.
- When the pass is complete, update summary, one-pager, launch dependencies, checkpoint and verification; regenerate the business plan with the project-local compiler. Preserve historical board/panel/pitch evidence. Put any evidence-based board reassessment in a dated addendum using the original rubric; do not fabricate a new score. Keep simulated demand and observed sales visibly separate.
- Verify arithmetic, provenance and assumptions, internal links, consistent scope/pricing/hours, unchanged historical evidence, and compiled-document freshness. Run the existing reporting tests if model inputs or scripts change. Record the compiler's expected `Not yet` exit 1 separately from verification failures; do not lower its 25% bar or replace simulated answers with real sales.
- Completion of the **local pass** means usable drafts, traceable economics or clearly named missing inputs, a coherent acquisition experiment and a verified packet. Completion of the **business test** requires actual customer actions; preparing documents does not satisfy it.
